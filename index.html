Services

Independent advice and hands-on engineering for companies that build innovative products.

  • Strategic consulting

    Advice on technology choices, product roadmaps, business strategy and IP protection, based on years of experience building and leading technology companies.

  • Engineering

    Design and development support for demanding products: concept work, detailed design, prototyping and translation to production. In-house prototyping capabilities allow for short hardware development cycles.

  • 3D printing expertise

    In-depth knowledge of additive manufacturing, including metal laser powder bed fusion: process selection, design for 3D printing and industrialisation.

  • Medical device development

    Guidance through the development of medical devices, from clinical need and design to the path towards market approval. Experience with standard and patient-specific medical devices in all risk classes and through various regulatory pathways (CE/MDR, FDA 510(k)).

  • Intellectual property

    Support in building and protecting your technology position.

    • Prior art search
    • Patent drafting
    • IP strategy
    • Licensing

About

CSEC is the consultancy and engineering firm of Peter Mercelis.

Peter holds an MSc and a PhD in engineering from KU Leuven. In 2008 he co-founded LayerWise, a company specialised in metal 3D printing for medical, dental and industrial applications, and led it as CEO.

Since 2014 Peter has co-founded and supported several 3D printing and medtech startups. He is a named inventor on 15 patent families in various technical and medical domains. View the patents and patent applications on Google Patents.
